# Image post style — GrowthStackr [EXEMPLAR — the quality bar, not your style]

> Structure exemplar. The user's own examples win, field by field. Output is always in the
> user's brand, never in GrowthStackr's.

## Structure & length
Hook line + 3–5 short body lines + one CTA. The image carries a single idea (a diagram, a
one-line quote, a before/after), not a busy infographic.

## Hook / opening style
One provocative or useful line. "Renting pipeline is why marketing feels like a treadmill."

## Formatting rules
Line breaks, sentence case, ≤1 emoji (usually 0). (GrowthStackr's own images use a coral accent,
cream background and a serif headline — that is their brand, not a rule for yours.)

## Do / Don't
- Do: one idea, one accent, lots of whitespace, a real number with a source.
- Don't: stock people, gradient blobs, banned words (`brand-guidelines.md` if present, plus the
  forbidden list in `references/copy-frameworks.md`).

## Example references
Ask the user in-run for 1–2 posts they like (links, screenshots, or pasted text). Extract
concrete rules from them (hook style, line rhythm, length, hashtag load, emoji policy, CTA
shape) and apply those on top of this bar.
