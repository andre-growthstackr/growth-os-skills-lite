# Copy frameworks — hook, body, CTA + language rules

Applied in Step 2 (write the core) and Step 4 (language + humanizer). Use `/copywriting` for the deeper frameworks if it is installed; this file is the working checklist so the skill produces strong copy even standalone.

---

## The hook (line 1)

The hook exists to earn line 2. It must survive the channel's truncation cut (see `channel-norms.md`). No warm-up, no "In today's fast-paced world".

Proven patterns — pick the one the angle fits:

- **VoC pain, stated plainly.** Say the reader's problem in their own words. "You keep hearing 'we need AI' and getting zero ROI."
- **Specific number.** "95% of AI pilots return nothing." A concrete figure beats a vague claim (must be sourced).
- **Contrarian claim.** Reverse the common advice. "Stop writing prompts. Write systems."
- **Open loop.** Promise a payoff the body delivers. "The reason your content sounds like everyone else's isn't your writing."
- **Named enemy / false belief.** Call out the thing the reader has been sold. "Prompt hacks don't move the needle. Context does."

Bad hooks: questions the reader can answer "no" to and scroll past, generic setups, throat-clearing, three adjectives in a row.

## The body

- **One idea per line.** Line break after every sentence. No text walls.
- **Concrete nouns over adjective stacks.** Tool names, numbers, exact outcomes. "Cut research from 3 hours to 20 minutes," not "dramatically faster".
- **Build from the reader's words.** Use the VoC phrases pulled in Step 0. Their language, not the brand's.
- **One central analogy at most.** Make the abstract physical. Don't chain metaphors.
- **Handle the obvious objection inside the body.** "A prompt library gives you prompts. These are the files that feed every prompt."
- **Earn each line.** If a line doesn't add information or momentum, cut it.

## The CTA

Match the goal chosen in Step 1:

- **Comment-keyword (lead-magnet funnel):** `Kommentera "ORDET" så skickar jag [lead magnet].` — payoff in the same sentence. This drives the comment → DM → lead magnet → offer flow. Line placement per channel (post CTA on LinkedIn/Instagram; reply on X).
- **Soft engagement:** a real question the reader wants to answer. Not "thoughts?" — something specific to the angle.
- **Profile/link click:** name what they get on the other side, not "learn more". On LinkedIn/X, put the link in the first comment/reply and point to it.
- **None:** pure value post — end on the strongest line, no ask.

Never "follow for more" begging. Never a CTA that outweighs the value above it.

## Language rules (Step 4)

- Correct grammar: särskrivningar, de/dem, subject–verb agreement.
- Conversational rhythm — contractions, direct address ("du"), reads like a person talking.
- Every line survives the "would I say this out loud?" test.
- Default fifth-grade readability unless the ICP is a specialist audience that expects the jargon.

## Forbidden AI patterns (hard stop — additive to `brand-guidelines.md`)

Strip all of these in the humanizer pass:

- **Inflated symbolism / grandiosity:** "In a world where…", "the landscape of…", "at its core".
- **Rule-of-three spam:** symmetrical triads ("faster, smarter, better").
- **Buzzwords:** delve, leverage, unlock, elevate, harness, seamless, robust, game-changer — and Swedish equivalents (dyk ner i, navigera, i en värld där, låt oss utforska).
- **Em-dash chains** and the "not only X — but Y" / "inte bara X — utan Y" negative parallelism.
- **Vague attributions:** "experts say", "studies show" without a source.
- **Hedging padding:** "it's worth noting that", "in order to", "at the end of the day".
- **Fabricated statistics.** Any number needs a source. No source → cut the number.

If `brand-guidelines.md` lists additional banned words or forbidden characters (e.g. no emojis), those apply on top of this list.
