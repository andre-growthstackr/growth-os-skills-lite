## Structure & length
Answer-first: the first paragraph answers the query in 2–3 sentences (citable). Then depth.
H2/H3 with one keyword focus per page. 1,500–2,500 words unless the search results want more or less.

## Hook / opening style
Direct answer, no throat-clearing. No "In today's fast-paced world".

## Formatting rules
Short paragraphs, one idea each. Sourced stats (number + URL) or cut. Information gain: add
own data/opinion/first-hand experience on top of the AI draft. Charts only where they clarify (see `references/writing-rules.md`).

## Do / Don't
- Do: E-E-A-T author signals, FAQ block, internal links to service/offer pages.
- Don't: fluff intro, unsourced numbers, banned words, rule-of-three padding.

## Example references
Ask the user in-run for 1–2 articles they like (links or pasted text). Extract concrete rules
from them (length, intro style, heading pattern, tone) and apply those on top of this bar.
