# Privacy Policy — Growth OS Lite

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Growth OS Lite is a set of skills (instruction files) published by GrowthStackr. It does not run
its own server, create accounts, or collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data. GrowthStackr
receives nothing when you use the skills.

**Where your data lives.** The skills read only what you make available: files in your Claude
project, files you upload or paste into a chat, and files in your working folder (Claude Code).
Results are delivered in the chat and as files you download or that are saved in your working
folder. Nothing is published, sent, posted, or synced anywhere by the skills.

**External tools are optional and permission-first.** Some skills can use an external connector
(MCP server) you have set up yourself, such as a web-scraping, SEO-data, analytics, or
ads-platform service. Before the first use in a run, the skill asks for your permission and names
the service, what it will be used for, and what it may cost. If you say no, the skill continues
with Claude's built-in tools. Data handled by those services is governed by your agreements with
them and their privacy policies. The skills never ask for, print, or store API keys or passwords.

**Your Claude host.** Claude.ai, Claude Desktop, and Claude Code process conversations and files
under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. Do not share secrets, personal data, or confidential
material you are not authorized to process there.
